Buying notes for this category

  • Ask for the selection software output for your duty point - catalogue capacities are nominal.
  • Confirm the coil tube thickness; thin tubes fail in three or four seasons in coastal air.
  • For clean rooms, get the filter class and the casing leak test result.
  • Check the AHU section drawings against your plant room before production.
