Buying notes for this category

  • 201 stainless in a coastal project will spot within a year; specify 304 and test with a magnet + acid.
  • Ask for the cycle test report on hinges - 50,000 cycles is the realistic threshold for commercial doors.
  • Order finish samples from the same plating batch you will buy; colour drift between batches is common.
  • Confirm screw gauge and length, since "with screws" means different things across suppliers.
