Buying notes for this category

  • Zinc-alloy bodies inside a brass-looking finish are the most common downgrade - ask for a cut sample.
  • Chrome below 8µm shows corrosion within a season in coastal installations.
  • Cartridge quality drives the warranty claim rate; specify the brand, not "high quality".
  • For utility valves, confirm the pressure test per batch and the epoxy coating thickness.
