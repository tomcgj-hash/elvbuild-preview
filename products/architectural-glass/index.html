What to put in the specification

  • Thickness build-up and the lamination interlayer (PVB/SGP, thickness)
  • Coating: Low-E type, U-value and SHGC targets for your climate zone
  • Edge work: polished, chamfered, drilled, and the tolerance on hole position
  • Fragmentation test result for tempered glass (particle count)
  • Packing in end-cap crates with ISPM-15 timber, and the breakage clause

Substitutions we check before quoting

  • Tempered glass cannot be cut later - freeze the sizes before production.
  • Ask for the heat-soak option on large facades; spontaneous breakage is rare but expensive.
  • Transport damage is the main risk. Agree the breakage allowance and photo evidence procedure up front.
  • Insulated units need the desiccant and seal brand confirmed; fogging is a seal failure, not a glass failure.
