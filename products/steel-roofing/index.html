What to put in the specification

  • Substrate: hot-dip galvanised zinc coating in g/m², and the steel grade (S250/S350)
  • Colour coating: polyester/SMP/PVDF, top-coat thickness in µm
  • Profile and rib height, and the covering width against the nominal width
  • For sandwich panels, the core (EPS/PU/rock wool) density and fire class
  • Warranty terms for colour fade and corrosion in your climate

Substitutions we check before quoting

  • Zinc coating below 120 g/m² will not survive a coastal roof; ask for the coating mass certificate.
  • PVDF against polyester is a 2-3x price difference and a 10-year versus 3-year fade difference.
  • Confirm the panel length limit for transport before designing the roof layout.
  • For sandwich panels, check the joint detail and the sealing tape supply.
