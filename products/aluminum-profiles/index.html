Extruded profiles, thermal-break windows and facades

Nanhai Dali is the birthplace of China's private aluminum extrusion industry and still holds the country's only national aluminum profile base title; Linqu in Shandong is the northern counterpart, and Anyi in Jiangxi the central one. What to put in the specification

  • Alloy and temper (6063-T5/T6), and the profile wall thickness in mm
  • Surface: anodising microns, powder coating microns, or PVDF
  • Thermal break: polyamide strip width and the U-value of the assembled window
  • Die ownership and whether your profile section is exclusive
  • Tolerance standard: GB/T 5237 against EN 755, and the flatness limit

Substitutions we check before quoting

  • Ask for the wall-thickness measurement on the actual profile, not the drawing - thin walls are the standard substitution.
  • Anodising thickness below 10µm will chalk within two years outdoors.
  • For window systems, buy one assembled sample window; the hardware and gasket interaction is where leaks start.
  • Confirm die cost, ownership and amortisation before the first order - it changes the year-two price.
