Five steps, one file per project

Sourcing fails in predictable places: the wrong county, an RFQ two factories read differently, a certificate for a different model, a container loaded without a plan. The process below exists to close those gaps.

First reply: 2 working days Sample parcel: consolidated Fee: agreed before start
01

You send a brief

Product, specification or reference sample, target quantity, destination market, certification required and your target price. A photo of what you buy today is usually worth more than a paragraph.

You getWe confirm scope in writing, including what we will not do.
02

We locate the belt, then the factory

The county that makes the product is not always the city your contact list suggests. We shortlist inside the right belt, then filter by capacity, export record, certification and willingness to quote at your volume.

You getA candidate list with location, product range and why each one made the cut.
03

Samples and normalised quotes

Factories are asked to quote the same specification so prices are actually comparable. Samples are collected, photographed and consolidated into one parcel instead of five separate courier bills.

You getOne price table, one sample parcel, and the specification gaps flagged in writing.
04

Inspection before money moves far

During-production and pre-shipment inspection against the approved sample: dimensions, functions, markings, packing, carton marks and the certificate actually issued for your model.

You getA written report with photos and measurements, plus a clear pass / fix / reject call.
05

Consolidation, documents, repeat orders

Goods from several belts loaded into one container against a plan you approve. Export documents, FOB or CIF, and a running file per factory so the second order is faster than the first.

You getShipping documents, loading photos, and a factory file you keep.
How we decide

Five rules we do not bend

Two trades only

We decline categories we do not know. A sourcing agent who quotes everything is a forwarder with extra steps.

Belt first, then supplier

Starting from a supplier list means you never learn what the market price really is. We start from where the product is made.

One specification, many quotes

Quotes only compare when the spec is identical. Normalising the RFQ is most of the work and most of the savings.

Certificates verified, not photographed

We check that the certificate number covers your model and is still valid. This is where ELV and fire product projects fail customs.

Nothing charged before scope is agreed

You see the fee and what it buys before work starts.

Tell us the product, the quantity and the market - we quote from the belt that makes it

Send one email with what you need. You get back a shortlist of factories with capacity, certification and a real price - typically within two working days.
