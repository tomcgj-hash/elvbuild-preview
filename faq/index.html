Working with us

Is the belt directory free?

Yes. Every profile, product page and region page is free, including the source links. We earn from sourcing projects.

How is the fee calculated?

Either a fixed project fee (matching, audit, inspection) or a commission on order value (full agency, where we negotiate and follow up until shipment). The model is named in the written scope.

How fast is the first reply?

Two working days for standard products. Engineered items such as switchgear or HVAC take longer because drawings have to circulate.

Do you take orders below MOQ?

We can, at a price. Small runs are possible on fasteners, mesh, tiles and cable; sanitary ware, doors, elevators and switchgear only make economic sense at volume.

Can you source something not listed on the site?

If it belongs to ELV systems or building materials, ask - the belt map covers more than the profiles published so far. If it is outside those two families we will decline.

Quality and verification

How do you verify the belt facts?

Only published sources are used: MIIT cluster lists, provincial and municipal government releases, industry associations and named media reports. Each profile lists them and unverifiable figures are omitted.

Do you check certificates?

Yes, and it is often the most valuable step. We confirm the certificate number covers the exact model, is issued to the exporting factory and is valid for the destination market.

What does an inspection cover?

Dimensions, functions, markings, packing, carton marks and label compliance against the approved sample. You receive a written report with photos and measured values.

What happens if goods fail inspection?

We stop the shipment, document the gap and put the fix to the factory. You decide: rework, replacement or rejection - we do not release goods you have not approved.

Payment, samples and shipping

How are samples handled?

Samples are ordered against your spec, consolidated into one parcel, photographed and labelled. Sample cost and freight are usually charged at cost.

Can you consolidate several suppliers?

Yes. Multiple suppliers, one container, one loading plan you approve before goods move.

Who books the shipment?

We can book and handle export documents on FOB or CIF terms, or work with your forwarder if you have one.

How do you handle payment?

Payment terms are part of the written scope and are agreed per project - typically a deposit against the order and the balance before shipment, with documents checked before release.
