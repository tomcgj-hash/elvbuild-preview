Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Guangzhou Hanting Information Technology Co., Ltd.

What we collect

When you submit the inquiry form or email us we receive the information you choose to send: your name, email, company, country, the requirement you describe, and any files you attach. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Why we collect it

Solely to answer your sourcing request: to shortlist factories, request quotations, plan samples and follow up on your project. We do not add you to a newsletter and we do not sell or rent contact data.

Who else sees it

To quote a product we must share the relevant part of your requirement with candidate factories - typically the specification, quantity and required certification. Your name and email are shared only when a factory needs to send documents directly, and never as a mailing list. Form submissions are delivered through a third-party form service to our mailbox; hosting and form processing providers act as data processors only.

How long we keep it

Project correspondence is kept for as long as the commercial relationship lasts and for a reasonable period afterwards for warranty and repeat-order purposes. You may ask for deletion at any time and we will remove enquiry data unless we are required to retain a specific document for tax or customs purposes.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies. If site analytics are enabled, they are configured to measure page traffic in aggregate without profiling individual visitors.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask for corrections, or ask us to delete it. Write to sales@sourcebridge.example and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Access to inquiry data is limited to the staff handling your project. We do not store payment card data - payments, when applicable, are handled through the terms stated in your written project scope.
