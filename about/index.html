Why belts instead of supplier lists

Most sourcing conversations start with a supplier list someone else assembled, which means the first question - what should this cost - can never be answered. Chinese manufacturing is organised by county and town far more tightly than by city: a single town can hold most of the country's capacity for one product, and the second supplier on your list is often buying from the first.

Starting from the belt gives you a price floor, a realistic MOQ and a shortlist of plants that genuinely compete. That is why this site is built around 46 belt profiles rather than a factory banner wall.

How the data is compiled

Every profile is assembled from published sources: the MIIT national SME specialised cluster lists (2022-2025 rounds), provincial and municipal government industry releases, industry associations and named news reports. Each profile links its sources at the bottom. Where a figure could not be verified, it is left out rather than estimated - a belt page with no number is more useful than one with a wrong number.

What we do for a buyer

Shortlisting inside the right county, normalising quotes to one specification, sampling, verifying that certificates actually cover your model, inspecting before shipment, and consolidating several suppliers into one container. Fees are agreed per project in writing. The directory itself stays free - it is how we introduce ourselves.

Three things we will not do

Quote a category we do not know

If your product sits outside ELV or building materials, we say so instead of learning on your order.

Publish a number we cannot source

No estimated outputs, no rounded-up enterprise counts, no borrowed figures from another directory.

Hide a factory problem

If an audit or inspection goes badly, you hear it from us before the goods move - with photos.

Tell us the product, the quantity and the market - we quote from the belt that makes it

Send one email with what you need. You get back a shortlist of factories with capacity, certification and a real price - typically within two working days.
