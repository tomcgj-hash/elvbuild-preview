Home/Services

Directory is free. The hands-on work is quoted per project.

No published hourly rate and no subscription. You tell us the scope, we quote it in writing, and nothing is charged before you approve. Every service below states what we do, what you receive and how long it takes.

01

Factory matching & vetting

We shortlist factories inside the correct industrial belt, then check capacity, export licence, certificates, main markets and willingness to quote at your volume.

You receiveA candidate list with location, product range, contact status and a price table normalised to one specification.
Typical timeline3-5 working days for standard products.
02

Factory audit (remote or on site)

Remote audit by video walk-through with photos, or an on-site visit from Guangzhou with interpreting: production lines, test equipment, warehouse, quality records and staffing.

You receiveA written audit report with photos, measured capacity and a list of open risks.
Typical timelineRemote: 2-3 days. On-site: scheduled with the factory, usually 1-2 weeks out.
03

Sampling & pre-production

We order samples against your spec, consolidate them into one parcel, photograph and label each one, and manage the specification discussion with the factory.

You receivePhysical samples plus a comparison sheet: what matches, what does not, what we recommend changing.
Typical timeline2-3 weeks depending on the belt and customisation.
04

Inspection

During-production and pre-shipment inspection against the approved sample: dimensions, functions, markings, packing, carton marks and label compliance.

You receiveA written report with photos, measured values and a pass / fix / reject recommendation.
Typical timelineBooked 3-7 days ahead of the shipment date.
05

Consolidation & shipping

Goods from several belt suppliers collected, checked against the packing list and loaded into one container against a loading plan. Documents and trade terms handled per shipment.

You receiveLoading photos, packing list verification, and the full document set for your clearing agent.
Typical timelineDepends on the sailing schedule; we confirm the cut-off before loading.
06

Certification & compliance check

We verify that the listing or type-test certificate names your model, is issued to the exporting factory and is still valid for the destination market.

You receiveA verification note per product family, with the certificate number and the issuing body checked.
Typical timeline1-2 working days, done before the order is placed.

How the fee works

Most projects are either a fixed project fee (matching, audit, inspection) or a commission on the order value (full sourcing agency, where we negotiate, follow up and stay on the file until the goods ship). We say which model applies after we see the brief, and put it in the written scope.

Never

Charged before scope is agreed. Charged for reading this site. Charged a percentage of savings we cannot evidence.

Always

A written scope with deliverables and timeline. Copies of every factory document we receive. Your data kept out of any list.

If we are wrong

If a belt turns out not to make your product, we tell you and refund the part of the scope we could not deliver.

Tell us the scope and we will price it

Send the product, volume and destination market. You get a written scope, a fee and a timeline - no obligation to proceed.
